TEXIOLDEXK MOBILE v2.1.2 VALIDATION

Validated in packaging environment:
- Controller JavaScript syntax passed
- Signalling wire regression validation passed
- Same-LAN native fallback simulation passed
- DXF2 frame transport reassembled 620,123 bytes from 51 deliberately reversed RTC chunks
- AndroidManifest.xml and all Android resource XML parsed successfully
- GitHub Actions YAML parsed successfully
- versionCode 23 and versionName 2.1.2 configured

Corrections in this release:
- Android WebView receives screen JPEGs as safe-size chunks and reassembles them out of order.
- A frame arriving immediately after approval is buffered until the viewer callback is installed.
- First-frame receive, render, assembly and decode diagnostics are reported to /monitor.
- The displayed Internet FPS is based on rendered frames instead of host capture FPS.
- Established P2P sessions attempt ICE restart after a disconnect.

Not validated here:
- APK/AAB compilation because Android SDK/Gradle were unavailable in this environment
- Physical Android WebView rendering and touch control
- Reliability across restrictive public networks without TURN
